About Me

Hi i am Leo Äijlä a Junior game dev from Finland.
I am a Junior 3d Hardsurface artist and Generalist with experience in gameplay programming in various game engines and some tehcnical art experience aswell.

Video games have always been importain to me as a player and being a creative i have always wanted to make them. Becoming a developer has always been my goal and im happy to dive in to the field.
